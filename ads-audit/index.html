Free video review or paid audit?

The free 5-minute video review is a quick first look: a short Loom on what stands out and where we would dig. It works even if you are not running ads yet. The audit is the full job. We go through the account with read access, verify what the tracking is actually recording, and write down what to fix in the order worth fixing it. That is why it is paid.

You are not buying a pitch. The fix list is written so your current team, or you, can act on it without us. If you continue into management, the $200 comes off your first month. If the audit doesn’t find at least $500/mo in waste or tracking errors, you get the $200 back. Either way, you keep the findings.

What gets checked, in order.

01

Conversion tracking accuracy

First, always. Whether conversions fire, fire once, and report the right value — across GA4, Google Tag Manager and the ad platforms. If this is wrong, every number below it is wrong too.

02

Account structure

How campaigns, ad groups and audiences are organised against how the business actually makes money, and where the structure is fighting the bidding rather than helping it.

03

Wasted spend

Where budget is going that cannot convert: irrelevant search terms, missing negatives, placements and audiences that spend without returning. This is usually the fastest money to recover.

04

Audience overlap and creative fatigue

Whether your own campaigns are bidding against each other, and whether performance decay is a creative problem or a targeting one. The distinction changes what you should do next.

05

Landing page and offer match

Whether the page delivers what the ad promised, and where the drop-off between click and conversion is a traffic problem versus a page problem.

What tends to come out of it.

The recurring finding is measurement: conversions double-counted, values missing, or events that never fired at all. In accounts we have rebuilt, cost per lead has gone from $0.82 to $0.18 on Google and $1.02 to $0.36 on Meta over 8 months for a DTC mattress brand (Central Asia market, lead-generation campaigns), and from $10 to $1 for an EV dealership. Non-running campaigns get diagnosed and restored as part of the same pass.

Full detail, with the dashboards behind most of these numbers, is in the case studies.
