It is paid, and that is deliberate.

A free audit is a sales call with a slide deck attached. It is built to find just enough wrong to justify a retainer, and it costs the agency nothing to give away. We charge $200 because the deliverable is real work: someone senior goes through the account, verifies what the tracking is actually recording, and writes down what to fix in the order worth fixing it.

You are not buying a pitch. The fix list is written so your current team, or you, can act on it without us. If you do continue into management, the $200 comes off your first month, so starting here costs nothing extra. If you don't, you keep the findings and we part ways.

What gets checked, in order.

01

Conversion tracking accuracy

First, always. Whether conversions fire, fire once, and report the right value — across GA4, Google Tag Manager and the ad platforms. If this is wrong, every number below it is wrong too.

02

Account structure

How campaigns, ad groups and audiences are organised against how the business actually makes money, and where the structure is fighting the bidding rather than helping it.

03

Wasted spend

Where budget is going that cannot convert: irrelevant search terms, missing negatives, placements and audiences that spend without returning. This is usually the fastest money to recover.

04

Audience overlap and creative fatigue

Whether your own campaigns are bidding against each other, and whether performance decay is a creative problem or a targeting one. The distinction changes what you should do next.

05

Landing page and offer match

Whether the page delivers what the ad promised, and where the drop-off between click and conversion is a traffic problem versus a page problem.

What tends to come out of it.

The recurring finding is measurement: conversions double-counted, values missing, or events that never fired at all. Accounts we have taken on after an audit have gone from $0.82 to $0.18 cost per lead on Google, $1.02 to $0.36 on Meta, and $10 to $1 in an enterprise funnel. Non-running campaigns get diagnosed and restored as part of the same pass.

Full case detail, with the dashboards behind each number, is on the results section of the homepage.
