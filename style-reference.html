Flight Plan

Four flights, thirty days

Flight 1 · Days 1–5

Build the asset

  • One-page site on Firebase (free hosting, you know the stack): who you help, 3 case studies with numbers, the audit offer, a booking link.
  • Lock pricing. Write the fixed deliverables list once — reuse forever.
  • Upwork profile live: niche it — "Google Ads & PMax specialist for e-commerce & beauty," not "digital marketer."
  • Build a 150-prospect list. Pick one niche you have proof in — beauty clinics and DTC e-com in UAE, AU, and Poland is the obvious wedge.
Flight 2 · Days 6–14

Open the pipeline — warm first

  • Message every past client and contact: Atelier Beauté, Center Matrasov, NKB Group, the RZ HR Studio thread. Ask each for one intro. Referrals close 5× faster than cold.
  • Ask Vicky at Setters: more hours available? Overflow clients?
  • Upwork: 10 proposals/week, sent within 15 minutes of the job posting. Lead with a specific observation about their listing, not a bio.
  • Cold: 20 personalized emails/day off your own pipeline. Offer a free 3-minute Loom mini-audit as the hook.
  • 2 LinkedIn posts/week. First one: the PMax tracking-bug story. Case stories outperform advice posts.
Flight 3 · Days 15–22

Convert

  • Target: 5–8 discovery calls booked from Flight 2 volume.
  • Sell 3–5 paid audits. Deliver each in 48 hours, over-deliver on the fix list.
  • End every audit call with one line: "Want me to just fix all of this? Here's the monthly." Convert at least one audit → retainer.
Flight 4 · Days 23–30

Stack & systemize

  • Close retainer #2 from the remaining pipeline.
  • Build the Looker Studio report template once — every client after this costs you 2 hrs/week, not 6.
  • Set the weekly ops rhythm: Mon optimize, Thu check-in, month-end report.
  • Check the scorecard. Decide on the quit trigger below.

Side Quest

A Bible trivia game will not print $3K in 30 days — apps monetize on years, not weeks. But it's a real asset: cap it at 2–3 hrs/week and treat it as audience + portfolio.

Do only this for now: share it in Bible-study Telegram groups and subreddits, add a simple donate link, and start collecting emails inside the app. An email list of engaged believers is worth more later than $40 of AdMob revenue now. It also doubles as your public proof of AI-builder skills. Fund it with $0 from this budget.

Quit Trigger

The plan works whether you quit today or in six weeks — but the risk profile is completely different. $100 is not runway; the Setters contract is your bridge, so protect it.

The trigger: hand in the resignation when signed monthly recurring hits $2,000 (Setters + one or two retainers) — or resign now and run Flights 1–2 inside your notice period so the clock starts while the paycheck's still coming.
