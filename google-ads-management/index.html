Most accounts we open are measuring the wrong thing.

Broken conversion tracking is the single most common problem we find in a new Google Ads account. Duplicate tags, conversions counted twice, form submissions that never fire, purchase values passed as zero. The account still reports a number, so nobody questions it — and every bid decision Google makes downstream is built on that number.

So tracking comes first. GA4 and Google Tag Manager get audited and rebuilt, server-side tagging goes in where the browser alone under-reports, and conversion actions get checked against what actually happened in the business. Only once the data survives that check do we touch campaign structure, bidding or budget. It is slower to start and it is the reason the numbers hold up later.

Questions worth asking before you hire anyone.

How much does Google Ads management cost?

Three tiers by ad spend. Under $5K/mo of spend it is $800/mo flat. From $5K to $25K it is 12% of spend with a $1,200/mo minimum. Above $25K the scope and fee are agreed together. Your tier is confirmed after the audit, once we have seen the account.

How fast will I see results?

Tracking fixes land within the first weeks. Meaningful cost-per-lead improvements typically take 60 to 90 days of data collection and optimization, because the bidding algorithms need clean data before they can learn. Anyone promising overnight results is guessing.

It is the normal starting point. The 48-Hour Ads Audit is $200 and is credited to your first month if you continue, so it costs nothing extra to begin there. If you would rather talk first, the 30-minute call is free.
