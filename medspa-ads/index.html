Where clinic ad budgets usually leak

Four problems worth checking first in any clinic account. Each has a different fix, and only some of them are ad problems.

01

Expensive leads

Lead cost creeps up when every location shares one campaign or the account optimises on the wrong signal. Campaigns get split by location and judged on cost per lead, so you can see where the money works and where it doesn’t.

02

No-shows

A cheap lead that never shows up isn’t cheap. Budget moves toward the campaigns whose leads actually book and arrive, which means agreeing a simple way for your front desk to share those numbers.

03

Leads not called back

Ads can’t pick up the phone, but reporting can make the gap visible. Lead volume and booked appointments are reported separately, so a follow-up problem doesn’t get mistaken for an ad problem.

04

Broken tracking

Pixel events firing twice, conversions missing, or treatment details ending up in URLs and event names. Tracking gets checked first, before any budget changes.

Questions clinics ask

How do you handle tracking without exposing patient health information?

The approach is to keep what reaches the ad platforms minimal: a generic “lead” or “booking” event rather than which treatment someone asked about, no form contents, and no page paths or parameters that reveal health details. Where it helps, events are routed server-side so you control exactly what is sent. This isn’t legal advice and doesn’t by itself make a practice HIPAA compliant: your compliance lead or counsel should sign off on the final setup for your clinic.

What does a medspa lead typically cost on Meta?

It varies by market, treatment and offer: an injectables special in a dense metro prices very differently from a body-contouring consult in a smaller town. As one real reference point, a US clinic account we run produced leads at $22.70 and $30.21 across two Connecticut locations. A realistic view for your market comes after we have seen the account, not as a promise.

How fast will we see results?

Tracking fixes come first and usually land early. How quickly lead cost improves depends on your budget, market and how much conversion data the account already has, so expect a learning period of weeks rather than days. Anyone promising overnight results is guessing.
