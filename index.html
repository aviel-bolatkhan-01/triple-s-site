Questions clients actually ask.

How fast will I see results?

Tracking fixes land within the first weeks. Meaningful CPL improvements typically take 60–90 days of data collection and optimization. Anyone promising overnight results is guessing.

What does it cost?

The 48-Hour Ads Audit is $200 flat. Monthly management runs in three tiers by ad budget: $800/mo flat under $5K spend, 12% of spend from $5K–$25K (minimum $1,200/mo), and custom above $25K.

What ad spend do I need?

There's no upper limit — the fee scales with the account: flat under $5K of spend, 12% above it. On small budgets, start with the audit instead: you'll know exactly what to fix before spending more.

Which platforms do you work with?

Google (Search, PMax, Demand Gen, YouTube), Meta, TikTok, LinkedIn and DV360 — with GA4, GTM and server-side tracking handled in-house.

How is Triple S different from a big agency?

Senior work on every account — no juniors, no hand-offs. And engineering-grade measurement: if the data is wrong, everything downstream is wrong, so tracking gets fixed first.

Do you require long-term contracts?

No. Engagements are month-to-month. If the numbers stop justifying the fee, you leave — no lock-in.

How do we start?

With the audit. Send your website and the platforms you run, then give us read access — you get a Loom walkthrough and a prioritized fix list within 48 hours. The $200 is credited to your first month if we work together. If you'd rather talk it through first, book a 30-minute call — same agenda, live.
