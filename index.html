Questions clients actually ask.

How fast will I see results?

Tracking fixes land within the first weeks. Meaningful CPL improvements typically take 60–90 days of data collection and optimization. Anyone promising overnight results is guessing.

What does it cost?

The 5-minute video review is free. The 48-Hour Ads Audit is $200 flat, refunded if it doesn’t find at least $500/mo in waste or tracking errors. Monthly management runs in three tiers by ad budget: $800/mo flat for $3K–$5K of spend, 12% of spend from $5K–$25K (minimum $1,200/mo, so the effective rate is above 12% below $10K of spend), and custom above $25K.

Monthly management starts at $3K/mo of ad spend, and there's no upper limit: the fee is flat from $3K to $5K and 12% above it. Under $3K/mo, start with the audit instead: you'll know exactly what to fix before spending more.
