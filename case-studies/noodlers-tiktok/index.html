Noodlers: TikTok campaigns for a food delivery brand

TikTok campaigns in a high-competition delivery market, with full-funnel tracking via GTM: spend tied to conversion data, not platform noise.

Client & context

Noodlers is a food delivery brand in the UAE. The work covers its TikTok advertising.

Client Noodlers Food delivery · UAE

The problem

Food delivery is a crowded category on TikTok: many brands chase the same hungry audience at the same hours, and platform-reported numbers alone don’t show which spend turns into orders.

What we did

Tracking

Full-funnel tracking set up through Google Tag Manager, so spend is tied to conversion data rather than platform-reported noise.

Results

The dashboard below shows one Dubai campaign: 122,320 impressions at $1.73 per conversion on a $247 budget.
