DTC mattress brand: an 8-month multi-channel rebuild

8 months of multi-channel optimization across Google, Meta and TikTok, on lead-generation campaigns in the Central Asia market. Google Ads CPL $0.82 → $0.18. Meta CPL $1.02 → $0.36. 200%+ lead growth.

Client & context

A direct-to-consumer sleep and home brand selling online, with lead generation on Google, Meta and TikTok. Lead-gen, Central Asia market.

Client DTC mattress brand Sleep & home · e-commerce
$0.14 per conversion

The problem

At the start, a Google Ads lead cost $0.82 and a Meta lead $1.02. The brief was more leads for less, across every channel the brand was already running.

What we did

Structure

Lead-generation Search campaigns on Google, run as dated flights so each period’s cost per lead can be compared, with Meta and TikTok alongside.

Bidding

Google Search campaigns bid on Maximize conversions with a target CPA.

TikTok

TikTok lead-gen flights re-run with CPM down from $0.46 to $0.35.

Results

  • Google Ads CPL $0.82 → $0.18 over 8 months.
  • Meta CPL $1.02 → $0.36.
  • 8,492 conversions at $0.14 each, 66.8% conversion rate, in the strongest Google campaign. These were lead-generation campaigns, so each conversion is a lead.
  • TikTok CPM $0.46 → $0.35.
  • 200%+ lead growth.
