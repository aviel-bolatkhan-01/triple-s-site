Enterprise media buying for Leroy Merlin and BIC

$30K+/mo across Google Ads, DV360 and Meta for enterprise retail, managed while embedded with MCA (Google Partner agency). Work contributed to the agency’s Google Partner badge.

$30K+per month across Google Ads, DV360, Meta
$10 → $1CPL, EV dealership, one month

Client & context

Leroy Merlin (home-improvement retail) and BIC (consumer goods), managed while embedded with MCA, a Google Partner agency. Triple S is not itself a Google Partner; the badge belongs to the agency.

Client Leroy Merlin Home improvement retail

The problem

Enterprise accounts spread budget across many product categories, regions and formats at once. Every line has to earn its share of spend, and reporting has to hold up for a brand-side marketing team.

What we did

Structure

Leroy Merlin’s Google Ads ran separate Search and display campaigns by product category and region, each with its own daily budget.

Formats

BIC ran on Display & Video 360 with in-stream and out-stream video line items, bought on CPM.

Results

  • $30K+/mo managed across Google Ads, DV360 and Meta.
  • EV dealership: CPL $10 → $1 in one month after a full account rebuild. Lead volume climbed so fast the client paused its outbound sales team until it could work the backlog.
  • 300% ROAS on Meta for a DTC brand.
